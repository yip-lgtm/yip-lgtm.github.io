核心事實

  • 【機制】 吸入 PM2.5 誘發氧化壓力與全身發炎，微粒可經肺泡入血並經神經路徑影響心血管。長期暴露連繫心梗、中風與早逝；兒童氣道較窄，阻塞比例更高。
  • 【量化】 2023 年環境 PM2.5、家居污染與臭氧導致 790 萬死亡〔640–940 萬〕、2.32 億健康生命年損失。少於 1% 人口年均達 WHO 5 µg/m³，36%（28 億）高於過渡目標 35；美國年標準 9 仍高於指引 5。
  • 【敏感度】 挪威低暴露情境（<10 µg/m³）中，只換濃度反應曲線即使早逝由基準 1,448 人〔502–1,497〕變 −91% 至 +104%；換資料來源另差 40%。平均 30 µg/m³ 時收窄至 −46% 至 +53%。
  • 【案例】 印度 31 城（2017）年均 PM2.5 為 33–194 µg/m³，早逝 80,447 人〔70,094–89,581〕，缺血性心臟病占 47%；損失 901.9 億美元〔880.2–924.1 億〕；2024 年政策僅改善早逝 1.01%。
  • 【案例】 中國 2019–2035：SSP1-2.6 早逝降 15.4%〔6.6–20.7%〕、SSP5-8.5 降 8.4%，SSP2-4.5 升 10.4%〔5.1–20.5%〕、SSP3-7.0 升 18.1%〔13.0–28.3%〕；老齡化是首位推升因素。
  • Mechanism. Inhaled PM2.5 triggers oxidative stress and systemic inflammation; particles cross the alveoli into the bloodstream and act through neural and endocrine pathways on cardiovascular function. Long-term exposure is linked to myocardial infarction, stroke and premature death; children have narrower airways and proportionally larger obstruction.
  • Magnitude. In 2023, ambient PM2.5, household pollution and ozone were linked to 7.9 million deaths (6.4–9.4 million) and 232 million healthy years of life lost. Fewer than 1% of people met the WHO annual level of 5 µg/m³; 36% (2.8 billion) exceeded even the least stringent interim target of 35 µg/m³, while the US annual standard is 9 µg/m³.
  • Sensitivity. In a Norwegian low-exposure setting (<10 µg/m³), changing only the concentration–response curve shifted attributable deaths from a reference 1,448 (95% CI 502–1,497) by −91% to +104%; changing exposure and health data added a 40% spread. At mean exposure up to 30 µg/m³ the range narrowed to −46% to +53%.
