主題 / Topic

車底爆震最常傷及足部：離地距、V形船底與延遲截肢的證據鏈

Underbody blast disproportionately injures the foot; this report traces clearance, hull geometry and delayed amputation.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【臨床證據】2010–2014年共有1,887名傷者及132名死亡軍人。超過90%的AIS 2+傷者同時有骨骼骨折；足部骨折佔傷者傷害13%，脛腓骨折佔死亡者傷害10%。

[Clinical evidence] Of 1,887 wounded and 132 killed personnel, over 90% of AIS 2+ casualties sustained fractures; foot fractures represented 13% of wounded injuries, and tibial-fibular fractures 10% of fatalities.

  • 【機制】底板受爆後急升、變形，將高幅短時軸向載荷傳至足踝。18個屍體腿樣本中12個出現跟骨、距骨、腓骨或脛骨骨折；5,931 N配合10.8 m/s撞擊速度對應50%失去行動能力風險。

[Mechanism] A rapidly rising floor transmits high-amplitude axial loads to the foot and leg; 12 of 18 cadaver legs fractured, with 50% incapacitation risk at 5,931 N and 10.8 m/s.

  • 【替代解釋／敏感度】結果不能全歸因於離地距：不同坐姿不改變假人指標，卻會改變真腿傷勢。另一模型顯示IMPAXX地板墊只在撞擊速度低於17.5 m/s時顯著降低主要跟骨傷風險；更高速度下效益有限。

[Alternative explanation and sensitivity] Results cannot be attributed to clearance alone: posture changes real-leg injury even when dummy metrics do not move. Another model shows the IMPAXX floor mat significantly reduces major calcaneal injury risk only below 17.5 m/s, with limited benefit at higher velocities.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【具體案例】2,996名下肢重傷軍人中，308人延遲截肢，即10.3%。舟骨、距骨及跟骨骨折者的延遲截肢率分別為36.2%、30.0%及28.1%；跟骨骨折兼神經損傷的截肢勝算比達41.74。

[Case study] Delayed amputation occurred in 10.3% of 2,996 severe lower-extremity injuries; delayed amputation rates were 36.2%, 30.0% and 28.1% for navicular, talar and calcaneal fractures respectively, and calcaneal fracture with nerve injury carried an odds ratio of 41.74.

  • 【推論】車型驗證應直接量度底板載入速度，而不能只比較裝甲厚度；17.5 m/s可作地板墊效益的明確測試邊界。短身材及女性乘員亦需要獨立評估，不能以單一男性樣本代表。

[Inference] Vehicle tests should measure floor-loading velocity rather than armour thickness alone, and 17.5 m/s marks an explicit test boundary for floor-mat benefit. Shorter stature and female occupants also need independent evaluation rather than a single male sample.

  • 【量化】截至2008年7月，GAO記錄MRAP採購已獲逾220億美元、涉及逾15,000輛車，但僅約6,600輛已投入前線。規模可稽核，卻不足以證明傷亡效益或全生命週期性價比。

[Quantification] By July 2008, the GAO recorded more than $22 billion appropriated for over 15,000 MRAPs, but only about 6,600 had been fielded. This scale is auditable, yet it does not establish casualty benefit or life-cycle cost-effectiveness.

簡評 / Commentary

臨床分佈、人體載荷試驗及延遲截肢數據方向一致，機制證據可信。車型幾何的邊際效益仍未充分量化；本題未找到「每增加一英寸離地距可減少多少傷亡」的可引用係數。另有二手評論聲稱MRAP的傷亡效益不及中型裝甲車，但其底層研究未能核實，故未採納。

Clinical, cadaver and outcome evidence point in a consistent direction, so the mechanistic evidence is credible. The marginal benefit of vehicle geometry is still not fully quantified; no citable coefficient was found for casualties prevented per additional inch of clearance. A secondary claim that MRAPs are less effective at saving lives than mid-size armoured vehicles was also excluded because the underlying study could not be verified.
