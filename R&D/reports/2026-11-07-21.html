綠氫電解槽硬件通縮：報價跌 60% 為何未變平氫

Electrolyser Hardware Deflation: A 60% Price Drop That Did Not Cheapen Green Hydrogen

2026-11-07 21:00 UTC · 1000 中文字 · 7943 bytes · 4 來源 · 通過品質閘

主題 / Topic

中國鹼性電解槽招標均價已跌至約 US$75/kW，較 2022 年低 60%，而全球年產能 58–61 GW 對 2025 年實際產出不足 5 GW。本文回答：硬件通縮為何沒有拉低每公斤綠氫，並量化電價、效率與堆疊價各自對 LCOH 的傳導幅度。範圍限於已量產的鹼式與 PEM，不含 SOEC、AEM 等未量產路徑。

Why a 60% collapse in electrolyser stack prices did not make green hydrogen cheap: the arithmetic behind electricity price, efficiency and stack cost, limited to commercial alkaline and PEM systems.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】過剩產能製造價格戰，不是需求不足：IEA 統計全球電解槽年製造產能由 2024 年 46 GW/yr 升至 2025 年近 58 GW/yr，但 2025 年實際產出估計不足 5 GW；中國產能逾 35 GW/yr 對 2025 年 4.7 GW 訂單（逾 90% 內銷），鹼性堆疊招標價跌穿 US$80/kW。替代解釋已排除：中國 2025 年新裝機佔全球近四分之三、期末佔全球已裝機約三分之二，需求端反而最強。

Manufacturing overcapacity, not weak demand, drives the price war: global capacity rose from 46 GW/yr in 2024 to nearly 58 GW/yr in 2025 while actual output stayed below 5 GW, and China booked only 4.7 GW of orders against over 35 GW/yr of capacity.

  • 【量化】成本差距在 CAPEX 口徑，不在堆疊價：IEA 已裝機電解槽 CAPEX（含 BoP、工程採購施工與不可預見費）2025 年中國 US$820/kW、其他地區 US$2,200/kW，約 2.7 倍；但 World Bank 指堆疊不足總 CAPEX 的一半，故堆疊降價對每公斤成本的傳導被稀釋，最大降本空間在 BoP、施工與系統整合。

The cost gap lives in installed CAPEX, not in the stack: USD 820/kW in China versus USD 2,200/kW elsewhere in 2025, while the stack is less than half of total CAPEX and balance of plant alone takes 30-60% of direct CAPEX, diluting any stack price pass-through.

  • 【量化】電費是主導變量：World Bank 基準（52 kWh/kg、$50/MWh PPA）下，電力成本單項已達 $2.60/kg；PPA 每升 $10/MWh，LCOH 升約 $0.52/kg（WB 原文；自算核對：52 kWh/kg × $10/MWh ÷ 1000 = $0.52/kg）；電費佔 LCOH 50–70%。

Electricity dominates: at 52 kWh/kg and a $50/MWh PPA the power component alone is $2.60/kg, each $10/MWh of PPA adds about $0.52/kg to LCOH, and power is 50-70% of total cost.

  • 【敏感度】效率紅利與電價紅利同量級，兩者不能互相取代：World Bank 現況 50–55 kWh/kg、研發路徑目標改善 20–30%、熱力學極限 33.4 kWh/kg（HHV 39.4）。按基準 52 kWh/kg、$50/MWh 自算：效率改善 20% 至 41.6 kWh/kg → 電力成本 $2.08/kg（減 $0.52/kg）；達極限 $1.67/kg（減 $0.93/kg）。同期一次 $10/MWh 的 PPA 變動推高 LCOH $0.52/kg，與整個 20% 效率紅利相當。

Efficiency gains and power-price moves are the same order of magnitude: reaching a 20% efficiency gain or the 33.4 kWh/kg thermodynamic limit cuts the power bill by $0.52-0.93/kg, the same scale as a single $10/MWh PPA step, so neither lever substitutes for the other.

  • 【案例】低價堆疊不等於可運行的電廠：World Bank 記錄中石化庫車項目實際輸出僅約預期三分之一；2025 年 11 月 40 家中國製造商聯署健康發展倡議，承諾終止低於成本投標與誇大性能；IEA 記錄 2026 年 3 月國富氫因虧損警示增發股本。

Cheap stacks do not make a working plant: the Sinopec Kuqa project produced roughly a third of expected output, and in March 2026 GuofuHee raised discounted equity after loss alerts.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】設備商是被硬件通縮淘汰的一方：IEA 指 58 GW/yr 產能中約 36% 屬只做氫能的公司，對需求波動敞口最高，已出現破產、重組與併購；中國自 2021 年大量新增進入者，惟 2025 年得標者不足三分之一。

Equipment makers are the losers of their own deflation: 36% of the 58 GW/yr capacity sits with hydrogen-only firms most exposed to demand swings, and fewer than one third of new Chinese entrants won orders in 2025.

  • 【推論】項目槓桿在電價與容量因子，不在報價：World Bank 明言高電價令低 LCOH 幾乎不可能，無論效率與融資如何優化；採購與投資判斷應看 PPA 與容量因子承諾，堆疊單價只是次要變數。

The project lever is the PPA and capacity factor, not the stack price: high power prices make low LCOH virtually impossible regardless of efficiency or financing.

  • 案例：交付責任由設備商轉向 BoP 整合：中石化鄂爾多斯 2025 年 9 月招標 48 套鹼性堆疊（每套 1,000–1,500 Nm³/h、約 5–7 MW），均價約 $75/kW（含稅、不含 BoP），較 2022 年低 60%，由隆基、三一與華電工程中標；開發商須自行整合空分、淨化與冷卻，設備商失去打包議價槓桿，亦新增交付整合風險。

A concrete case on the value chain: the Sinopec Ordos tender covered 48 alkaline stacks at about $75/kW excluding balance of plant, leaving developers to integrate gas separation, purification and cooling themselves.

  • 【推論】成本下降不會靠硬件通縮實現：IEA 中國 2030 STEPS 假設電解槽 CAPEX 降至 US$680/kW（其他地區 US$1,700/kW），降幅遠小於堆疊報價跌幅；已承諾與高潛力項目由 10 Mt 跌至 2030 年略高於 6 Mt，約 240 GW（佔全球管線 75%）投產機會低或不明朗——本輪是產能爆發，不是需求爆發。

Cost declines will not come from hardware deflation: China's 2030 STEPS electrolyser CAPEX of USD 680/kW falls far less than stack prices did, while 75% of the global pipeline has low or uncertain prospects by 2030.

Confidence is moderate: the two primary sources are independent, but their capacity figures differ slightly, the Kuqa output claim is not independently auditable, and post-March-2026 tender prices are not yet documented.
