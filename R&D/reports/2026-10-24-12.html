機床油霧「合規」但工人仍然發病：5 與 0.5 mg/m³ 之間的 10 倍落差

Compliant but Ill: The 10-Fold Gap Between 5 and 0.5 mg/m3 for Machine Mist

2026-10-24 12:00 UTC · 1052 中文字 · 9188 bytes · 8 來源 · 未評

主題 / Topic

美國 OSHA 至今用 5 mg/m³ 的一般礦物油霧 PEL 判定機床冷卻液霧合規，NIOSH 對金屬切削液（MWF）的推薦限值只有 0.5 mg/m³，而 ACGIH 的 TLV 明文寫「不含金屬切削液」。本報告問：這個 10 倍落差為何存在、霧量由哪些加工參數決定、為何合規工廠裏工人仍出現倍數的皮膚炎與工作性哮喘。範圍限美英機加工車間。

US OSHA still judges machine-coolant mist compliant against a generic 5 mg/m3 mineral-oil PEL, while NIOSH's limit for metalworking fluids is 0.5 mg/m3 and ACGIH's TLV explicitly excludes MWF. Why does the gap persist, which machining parameters set the mist rate, and why do compliant shops still show doubled dermatitis and work-related asthma?

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】油霧不是「切出來的」，是流體加刀具運動生成的。NIOSH 在全包覆加工中心做 2×2×3 因子實驗：刀具是否真的切除金屬完全不影響霧量；面銑霧濃度隨主軸轉速的 2 次方上升，立銑是 3.5 次方。

Mist comes from fluid plus tool motion, not from cutting. In NIOSH's factorial trial, whether the tool removed metal made no difference; mist rose with spindle speed to the 2nd power (face mill) and 3.5th (end mill).

  • 【機制】10 倍落差不是科學未決，是量度對象錯配。OSHA 的 5 mg/m³ 只覆蓋「straight oil」礦物油霧；佔業界 80–90% 的水溶性 MWF 在聯邦層面根本沒有適用限值。原因是 MWF 屬配方混合物，含細菌、內毒素、胺類與 isothiazolinone 生物防劑，總質量限值量不到它們；1993 年 UAW 已請願設 0.5 mg/m³ 標準，至今未果。

The gap is a mismatched measurand, not unresolved science. OSHA's 5 mg/m3 covers only straight mineral-oil mist; water-based MWF, 80-90% of use, has no applicable federal limit. MWF is a formulated mixture carrying bacteria, endotoxin, amines and isothiazolinones that a total-mass limit cannot see; UAW petitioned for 0.5 mg/m3 in 1993.

  • 【劑量—反應】2001 年一間機加工廠 3 名機工因過敏性肺炎住院。以氣膠地圖劃分 30 例病例：低曝崗位 4%、中曝 19%、高曝 34%；裝集塵器後霧量降約 75%，未受控輸送線附近反升約 20%。

Three machinists were hospitalised with hypersensitivity pneumonitis at one plant in 2001. Aerosol mapping of 30 cases gave 4% in low, 19% in medium and 34% in high-exposure jobs; collectors cut mist about 75%, an uncontrolled conveyor line raised it about 20%.

  • 【排除替代解釋】「本來體弱或吸煙所致」不成立：學徒研究起點時機工與對照無差異，2 年後支氣管反應性增幅才有差（機工為對照 2 倍，p=0.05），剔除起點 PC20<8 者後結果不變；密西根調查中吸煙、從不吸煙與已戒煙者的症狀差異同樣存在。

Pre-existing frailty or smoking is ruled out: apprentices did not differ at baseline and diverged over two years (machinists double the control increase, p=0.05), unchanged after excluding low baseline PC20; in Michigan the symptom gaps held for current, never and ex-smokers.

  • 【敏感度】同一批數據換基準即翻轉：13 家工廠 68 個全班次樣本 TWA 平均 0.46 mg/m³、峰值 3.65 mg/m³。以 OSHA 5 mg/m³ 判定全部合規；以 NIOSH 0.5 mg/m³ 判定則平均 0.92 倍、峰值 7.3 倍；若改用 ACGIH 5 mg/m³ 或 1998 年前的 10 mg/m³ STEL 基準，結論都是「普遍合規」。10 倍落差只在以 NIOSH 為基準時成立。

Swap the benchmark and the verdict flips: 68 full-shift samples in 13 shops averaged 0.46 mg/m3 (peak 3.65). Against OSHA's 5 mg/m3 all comply; against NIOSH's 0.5 mg/m3 the mean is 0.92x and peak 7.3x; against ACGIH's 5 mg/m3 or the pre-1998 10 mg/m3 STEL, all comply again.

  • 【事實】暴露低於 NIOSH REL 仍有症狀。NIOSH 在飛機發動機廠評估 407 名工人，暴露組手腕前臂皮膚炎 PR 2.59、哮喘症狀 PR 1.49、工作相關哮喘 PR 2.10，三者 95% CI 均不含 1。

Symptoms rose even below the NIOSH REL: NIOSH's assessment of 407 workers at an aircraft-engine plant found dermatitis PR 2.59, asthma PR 1.49 and work-related asthma PR 2.10, all CIs excluding 1.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】密西根州 1988–1994 年收到 86 件 MWF 工作性哮喘通報，MWF 是全州第二大病因；跟進檢查 37 家設施、訪談 755 人，只有 1 家超出油霧標準，但約 20% 的同事有每日或每週疑似工作性哮喘症狀。合規與安全在此明確脫鉤。

In Michigan, 86 MWF asthma reports made MWF the state's second cause; at 37 inspected sites with 755 workers interviewed, only one plant exceeded the mist limit, yet about 20% of colleagues had daily or weekly suspected work-related asthma.

  • 【事實】三個工程槓桿有實測數字：停機並關閉供液令區域霧濃度降 80%；舊輸送線加裝改良圍封降 87%；全包覆新線的個人與區域採樣值約為舊線一半。NIOSH 因此建議集塵器隨機啟停並強制使用圍封。

Three engineering levers carry measured gains: machining and coolant off cut area mist 80%; improved retrofit enclosure cut it 87%; a fully enclosed new line sampled about half the old line. NIOSH recommends collectors that start with the machine and enforced enclosure.

  • 【推論】製程參數本身就是暴露控制：主軸轉速下調 20%，依 2 與 3.5 次方外推，面銑霧量降約 36%、立銑降約 54%，可能比換過濾器更省。但外推只由鋁件立式加工中心實驗支撐；磨削與鑽孔的實測暴露反而高於車削與銑削，方向不可一概而論。

Process parameters are exposure control: a 20% spindle-speed cut implies about 36% (face mill) and 54% (end mill) less mist, possibly cheaper than new filters. But the exponents come from one aluminium machining centre, and grinding and drilling measured higher than turning and milling.

  • 【推論】受影響人群不小：NIOSH 指每年有 120 萬金屬加工工人接觸 MWF。英國工程業調查顯示水溶性槽液細菌中位 109,000 CFU/mL、內毒素中位 8,039 EU/mL，多數廠的流體管理未達良好作業水平。若改以 NIOSH 0.5 mg/m³ 重設通風基準，風機與過濾規格需整體重算。

The exposed population is large: NIOSH counts 1.2 million metalworkers exposed to MWF each year. UK survey sumps averaged 109,000 CFU/mL bacteria and 8,039 EU/mL endotoxin, with fluid management generally below good practice.

簡評 / Commentary

最紮實的一環是劑量—反應與工程控制：同一廠的氣膠地圖、三個控制手段的實測降幅、407 人與 755 人的現場樣本，數字可覆核。較弱的是「10 倍落差」的框法：OSHA 與 NIOSH 量度不同分級的氣溶膠，不能純粹相除當成監管漏洞倍數，報告已標明此條件。本題未找到可引用的 OSHA 專項執法金額、MWF 專屬 ACGIH TLV 原文與 MOF 類風濕性肺病發病率，三者不補猜測數字。下一步：向工廠索取逐工序 TWA 與供液壓力以重算排風。

The dose-response and control evidence is strongest: one plant's aerosol map, measured reductions for three controls, and field samples of 407 and 755 workers. The 10-fold framing is weaker, since OSHA and NIOSH grade aerosols differently and the ratio is conditional. We found no citable OSHA penalty amount, no ACGIH MWF-specific TLV text and no incidence figure for machine operator's lung, so none is invented here.
