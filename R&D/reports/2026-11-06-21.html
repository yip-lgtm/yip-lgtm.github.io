AI 內容回灌訓練集：崩潰門檻、修正法與一個已量化的臨界點

When AI Text Feeds Back Into Training: Collapse Thresholds, Fixes, and a Measured Tipping Point

2026-11-06 21:00 UTC · 1060 中文字 · 8232 bytes · 6 來源 · 未評

主題 / Topic

本報告回答：AI 生成的文字回到網絡、再被用來訓練下一代模型，會否愈學愈笨？答案不是必然，而是有可量化的門檻與條件——崩潰與否取決於真實資料有沒有被保留，以及一個比例臨界點。本文給出同場景對照的實驗數字、崩潰的因果鏈，以及對資訊生態與算力開支的直接代價。

This report asks whether AI-generated text returning to the web and then training the next model generation makes models progressively dumber. The answer is conditional, with measurable thresholds: collapse depends on whether real data is preserved and on a ratio tipping point. It gives same-setting experimental numbers, the causal chain, and the direct cost to the information ecosystem and to compute budgets.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】崩潰先吃掉尾巴，不是先令模型全面變笨：低概率事件因取樣機率低而最先消失——某狀態機率 q ≤ 1/M（樣本量 M）時期望出現 qM < 1 次，取樣後原分佈支撐集收縮，模型於是自信重複主流，稀有但真實的知識最先沒了。替代解釋已排除：不是首輪合成資料品質差（改變第一輪訓練量，各代曲線無質變），也不是模型變大就修好（更大模型反而放大崩潰）。

Collapse removes the tails first rather than making models uniformly dumber: a state with probability q ≤ 1/M is expected qM < 1 times, so sampling shrinks the support and rare-but-true knowledge vanishes first. Alternative explanations are excluded — not a bad first synthetic dataset, and bigger models do not fix it.

  • 【量化】保留真實資料可完全避免崩潰，同場景對照是 −0.07 對 +0.58：Gerstgrasser 等在 GPT-2 (9M) 測試，第 1 代交叉熵 1.82；第 4 代累加真實與合成資料為 1.74（−0.07），改成純合成替換則 2.39（+0.58），第 10 代替換升至 2.91（+1.09）；Llama-2 (126M) 為 1.71 → 1.59 對 2.23。取樣溫度 0.3 時第 10 代替換達 9.85（+8.04），遠甚於溫度 1。同一結論在擴散模型（GEOM-Drugs，下抽 4 萬構象）與 VAE 影像生成上成立。

Keeping real data avoids collapse entirely: GPT-2 (9M) cross-entropy 1.82 at t=1, then 1.74 (accumulate) versus 2.39 (replace) at t=4 and 2.91 at t=10; at temperature 0.3 the t=10 replacement reaches 9.85.

  • 【量化】崩潰不需要大量合成資料，1% 已鎖死 scaling law：Dohmatob 等證明合成資料只佔訓練集 1% 時，更大數據集不再提升性能；其隨機投影近似下更大模型會放大崩潰。現實更急：Pangram Labs 測得 2026 年 6 月網絡語料 27.5% token 被判為 AI 生成（兩年前 10.1%），8 月升至 31.1%。

Collapse needs no flood of synthetic data: 1% of the corpus is enough to stop larger datasets from helping, and larger models can amplify it. In the wild, Pangram Labs measures 27.5% of June 2026 web tokens as AI-generated, up from 10.1% two years earlier and 31.1% by August.

  • 【敏感度】門檻隨人類預算、取樣溫度與評估方式移動，結論有明確範圍：WildAI 預訓練 800 個模型（19.9M–973M 參數）顯示，數據饑餓模型加 AI token 初期降低人類文本損失但效益迅速飽和並反轉為傷害，人類預算充足者則幾乎立刻變差；在 22.3% AI 比例的混合驗證集上，95.5% 的有害實驗看起來正常。結論成立於「真實資料被替換」且任務依賴稀有知識；保留真實資料時結論翻正。

The threshold moves with human budget, sampling temperature and evaluation design: across 800 models, AI tokens reverse from benefit to harm for data-starved models, and on a mixed validation set at 22.3% AI share, 95.5% of harmful runs look normal.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】合成內容正在變成算力稅：以 2026 年 8 月 31.1% 的 AI 比例，直接用未過濾網絡語料訓練要 1.6 倍算力才追得上只用人類子集（20 tokens/param），差距隨人類預算擴大；按預測比例，2027 與 2028 年底將升至 2.1 倍與 3.0 倍。

Synthetic text is becoming a compute tax: at August 2026's 31.1% AI share, training on unfiltered web text needs 1.6× the compute of training on its human subset, rising to 2.1× and 3.0× by the ends of 2027 and 2028.

  • 具體案例：WildAI 審計顯示現有品質過濾正在放大污染：Pangram Labs 公開 83B token 語料、800 個模型與程式碼（github.com/pangramlabs/WildAI）；審計發現 FineWeb 讓 AI 文件以 2.3 倍機率入選，DCLM 流程更達 9.8 倍，故建議人類與 AI 文本分開驗證。

A concrete case: Pangram Labs released the 83B-token WildAI corpus and all 800 models; FineWeb keeps AI documents 2.3× as often as human ones and DCLM 9.8×.

  • 具體案例：高風險語境的深偽多數無標籤：跨 Instagram、TikTok、X、YouTube 審計（10,722 帖、500 帖專家標註、控制上傳 10 款工具）發現，系統性風險語境中專家認定的深偽只有 33% 帶平台自動標籤，中位觀看量 16 萬次；帶標準 AI 溯源訊號的上傳只有 61% 被標籤，平台常在事後移除訊號。

A concrete case: across Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube, only 33% of expert-identified deepfakes in systemic-risk contexts carried a platform-applied AI label, at a median 160,000 views.

  • 【推論】損失落在稀有但要緊的那一端：罕見疾病、邊界工況、少數語言文獻正落在被移除的尾部；開源社群亦受直接衝擊——294 個倉庫、逾 200 萬個 PR/issue 的時間序列分析顯示，一次性貢獻者的合併率相對反事實下跌 18.18%。

The loss lands on the rare-but-important: rare diseases, edge-case conditions and minority-language literature sit in the tail that collapse removes; one-time open-source contributors' merge rates fell 18.18% against the counterfactual.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：高。三個獨立團隊在各自設定下重現「尾巴先消失」，Nature 論文經同行評審並有 2025-03-21 作者更正；WildAI 與兩份審計屬預印本，數字應視為待複核。爭議點在「崩潰是否不可避免」：2024 年後的累加與原點存檔結果顯示可逆，但 WildAI 顯示真實網絡的混合污染更難靠過濾解決。本題未找到針對醫療或科學檢索等高風險下游的崩潰量化實測，該缺口未以推測填補。下一步應追蹤公開語料的 AI 比例與過濾器選項。

Confidence is high on the core mechanism: three independent groups reproduce tail-first loss, and the Nature paper is peer reviewed with a published author correction. The WildAI study and both audits are preprints and should be treated as unreplicated. The contested point is whether collapse is inevitable, since accumulation and origin-archive results post-2024 show it is avoidable, while wild-web contamination is harder to filter. No quantified collapse measurement for high-stakes downstream systems was found, and no guess is substituted for it.
