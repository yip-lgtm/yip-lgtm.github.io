主題 / Topic

美國飲用水新規把PFOA、PFOS上限定為4.0 ng/L。問題是：嚴格門檻波及多少人口，成本多高？其MCLG為零，因EPA判定兩者可能致癌。本報告只計受監測系統，不把名單當全國抽樣。

The US drinking-water rule sets limits of 4.0 ng/L for PFOA and PFOS. The central questions are how many people are affected and what treatment will cost. The MCLG is zero because EPA considers both substances likely carcinogens. Monitored systems are not treated as a national random sample.

核心發現 / Key Findings

【機制】PFAS高度持久，難以被一般水處理破壞。去除須用吸附、離子交換或膜法，並處理再生殘渣。

PFAS are highly persistent and resist destruction in conventional water treatment. Removal requires adsorption, ion exchange or membranes, together with management of treatment residuals.

EPA的20州非目標監測中，1,260套PFOS系統超標。PFOA亦有1,577套系統超標。兩者分別服務1,250萬與1,440萬人。

Among non-targeted monitoring in 20 states, 1,260 systems exceeded the PFOS limit. Another 1,577 systems exceeded the PFOA limit. These served 12.5 million and 14.4 million people, respectively.

【替代解釋】成本或由行政核查推高。但EPA表格顯示處理支出最大，故行政成本不是主因。上述人口不可相加，因系統可同時超兩項。

An alternative explanation is that administrative oversight drove costs. EPA’s tables instead identify treatment expenditure as the largest component. The two population figures cannot be added because one system may exceed both limits.

【敏感度】門檻升至10.0 ng/L時，超標系統降至491及612套。服務人口降至約530萬及600萬，仍非零。

Under a 10.0 ng/L alternative, the numbers of exceeding systems fall to 491 and 612. The affected populations fall to approximately 5.3 million and 6.0 million, but do not vanish.

【敏感度】水源替代若免多數新建處理，支出或靠近14.36億美元下端。膜法或殘渣處置若較昂貴，則或接近16.72億美元上端。非隨機監測亦使全國人數可能被高估。

If source replacement avoids most new treatment, costs may approach the USD 1.436 billion lower estimate. Costlier membranes or residual disposal could push expenditure toward the USD 1.672 billion upper estimate. Non-random monitoring may also inflate nationwide population estimates.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

【推論】若按4.0 ng/L執行，受影響系統需降源或加設處理。EPA估計全國年化支出期望為15.49億美元。

Implementation at 4.0 ng/L would require affected systems to control contamination at source or add treatment. EPA estimates an expected nationwide annualized cost of USD 1.549 billion.

【具體案例】加州有38套PFOS、36套PFOA系統超標。賓州分別有66及72套。

A concrete case is California, where 38 PFOS systems and 36 PFOA systems exceeded the limits. Pennsylvania had 66 and 72 systems, respectively.

四州目標監測另涉及約570萬人口，但與前述人口可能重疊。

Targeted monitoring in the same four states covered about 5.7 million additional people, although these populations may overlap with those above.

【推論】2029合規可讓小系統改設備，卻把改善延後約五年。處置能力、替代水源與技術選擇可改變成本。

The 2029 compliance date gives smaller systems time to change equipment, but delays improvement by about five years. Disposal capacity, alternative water sources and technology choices can materially alter costs.

本題未找到可引用的死亡或疾病量化估計。

This investigation found no citable quantitative estimate of associated deaths or disease burden.
