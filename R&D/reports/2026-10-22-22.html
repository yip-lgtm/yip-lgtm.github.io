助聽干預為何「總體陰性、分層有效」：ACHIEVE 試驗 61.6% 效應的機制與邊界

Why a Null Hearing Trial Split by Risk: The 61.6% ACHIEVE Effect

2026-10-22 22:00 UTC · 981 中文字 · 8972 bytes · 6 來源 · 通過品質閘

主題 / Topic

大型隨機試驗對「治耳聾防失智」給出陰性結果，但同樣數據按風險分層後顯示強效應。本報告問：總體陰性與分層有效能否同時成立？機制為何？對公共資源分配意味著什麼？範圍限 ACHIEVE 試驗、WHO HEAR 模型與美國助聽器負擔數據。

A null overall result and a strong risk-stratified effect can coexist. What mechanism reconciles them, and what does it mean for public resource allocation? Scope: the ACHIEVE trial, the WHO HEAR model, US hearing-aid affordability.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 總體陰性 — ACHIEVE 試驗（Lancet 2023）977 人，3 年認知變化 −0.200 對 −0.202 標準差，差異 0.002（95% CI −0.077 至 0.081），p=0.96。
  • 【機制】第一段成立 — Reed 等（JAMA Intern Med 2025）：干預組 3 年後社交網絡比對照組多保留 1 人，孤獨感低 0.94（95% CI −1.78 至 −0.11）。「助聽器→溝通→社交連結」確實改變，缺失的是「社交連結→認知」。
  • 【機制】效應異質性 — ARIC 子組（238 人）3 年衰退速率為 de novo 子組（739 人）的 2.7 倍（−0.402 對 −0.151）；干預令前者衰退減 48%（差異 0.191，95% CI 0.022–0.360，p=0.027），後者無效應（−0.061，p=0.18）。排除的替代解釋：兩臂在同族群內隨機分配，差異不能來自族群本身；餘下是地板／天花板效應，需以風險模型檢驗。
  • 【機制】風險分層確認 — Pike 等（Alzheimer's & Dementia 2025）以 ARIC 2,692 人建模預測風險並套用 977 人：最高風險四分位組 3 年衰退減慢 61.6%（95% CI 33.7%–94.1%），交互 p=0.03；語言域 p=0.04，記憶與執行功能不顯著。
  • 【敏感度】 — 61.6% 來自事後四分位切分（約 244 人）與模型外推，非預先分層；主試驗敏感性分析 p_interaction=0.010。若改用不含認知測驗的簡化模型，或改以連續劑量反應描述，結論由「高風險亞組有效」退回「效應連續、無硬分界」；外推至全部 977 人或全體聽障人口則不成立。
  • 成本側不對稱 — Tordrup 等（Lancet Global Health 2022）模型：全球 ROI 14.81，低收入國家 −0.07（回報低於成本），高收入國家 36.59；每 DALY 迴避成本 US$1,633–2,161。MarkeTrak 2025（4,993 人，擁有者 1,173）處方助聽器中位價 US$1,560，OTC 僅 US$150。

Overall null. ACHIEVE (Lancet 2023, n=977): 3-year cognitive change −0.200 vs −0.202 SD, difference 0.002 (95% CI −0.077 to 0.081), p=0.96.

【機制/Mechanism】. Reed et al. (JAMA Intern Med 2025): one extra person kept in the social network at 3 years, loneliness −0.94 (95% CI −1.78 to −0.11) — the aid → communication → social connection step changed, the social connection → cognition step did not. ARIC (n=238) declined 2.7× faster than de novo (n=739) (−0.402 vs −0.151 SD); the intervention cut decline 48% in ARIC (diff 0.191, p=0.027), not in de novo (−0.061, p=0.18). Randomisation was within cohort, so the gap is not a cohort effect; a floor/ceiling effect remains. Pike et al. (Alzheimer's & Dementia 2025) modelled risk on 2,692 ARIC participants, applied to 977: top quartile declined 61.6% slower (95% CI 33.7%–94.1%), interaction p=0.03; language p=0.04, memory and executive function n.s.

【敏感度/Sensitivity】. Post-hoc quartile split (~244 people) plus model extrapolation, not prespecified; sensitivity analysis gave p_interaction=0.010. A simplified model, or a continuous dose-response instead of a cut point, gives "continuous effect, no hard boundary"; extrapolating to all 977 fails.

Cost asymmetry. Tordrup et al. (Lancet Global Health 2022): global ROI 14.81, but −0.07 in low-income vs 36.59 in high-income countries, US$1,633–2,161 per DALY averted. MarkeTrak 2025: median prescription aid US$1,560 vs US$150 OTC.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 市場事實 — 39% 有感知聽力困難者用處方助聽器，51% 用任何裝置（MarkeTrak 2025）；US$1,500 自付對逾半美國成年人屬災難性支出（Jilla 等 2020），US$250 對逾 90% 聽障者可負擔。
  • 【推論】資源重新排序 — 若效應只在高風險者成立，資源應由「全民普發」轉向「風險分層篩檢＋治療」。ACHIEVE 樣本 977 人中最高風險四分位約 244 人；把 61.6% 換算成人年延緩所需係數，本題未找到可引用來源。
  • 具體案例：低收入國家 — WHO HEAR 模型 172 國估算，低收入國家 ROI −0.07，即篩查加管理成本略高於收益，同模型在高收入國家為 36.59；全球「每 1 美元回報 16 美元」說法掩蓋所得分層落差。
  • 【推論】現場取樣 — ACHIEVE 四個 ARIC 現場站點（Forsyth County 北卡、Jackson 密西西比、明尼阿波利斯郊區、Washington County 馬里蘭）皆為社區樣本；用於公共衛生時，招募管道本身即高風險族群的取樣偏差來源。

Market fact. 39% of adults with perceived hearing difficulty use prescription aids, 51% some device (MarkeTrak 2025); US$1,500 out of pocket is catastrophic for over half of US adults (Jilla et al. 2020); US$250 is affordable to over 90%.

【推論/Inference】Resource reordering. If the effect holds only in higher-risk people, resources should shift from universal aid distribution to risk-stratified screening plus treatment. The top quartile is ~244 of 977; no published coefficient converts 61.6% slower decline into person-years.

Concrete case: low-income countries. The WHO HEAR model across 172 countries returns ROI −0.07 in low-income countries — screening plus management costs slightly more than it returns — against 36.59 in high-income; the global "US$16 per US$1 invested" framing hides the gradient.

【推論/Inference】Field sampling. All four ACHIEVE ARIC sites are community samples; recruitment is itself a sampling-bias source for high-risk groups.

簡評 / Commentary

  • 可信度：隨機試驗、全球模型、消費者調查三類證據互不依賴；但主試驗與分層分析共享同一批受試者，不能視為獨立複製。
  • 爭議：61.6% 屬事後分層且區間寬（33.7%–94.1%）；語言域顯著而記憶域不顯著，與「認知儲備」解釋一致但未直接測量儲備。
  • 缺口：未找到 ACHIEVE 助聽器購買率與每日使用時數的一手數據；未找到 OTC 助聽器隨機對照試驗的認知結果；無 61.6% 換算成人年的公開係數。
  • 下一步：追蹤 ACHIEVE 五年追蹤與暴露時數分層分析，並比較不同所得國家實施風險分層的成本。

Confidence. Three evidence chains (randomised trial, global model, consumer survey), but the trial and the stratified analysis share participants, so they are not independent replication.

Contested. The 61.6% is post-hoc with a wide interval (33.7%–94.1%); language is significant while memory is not, consistent with a cognitive-reserve explanation never directly measured.

Gaps. No first-hand data on ACHIEVE aid purchase rates or daily use hours; no RCT of OTC aids reporting cognitive outcomes; no published coefficient for 61.6% → person-years.

Next. Follow the ACHIEVE five-year follow-up and exposure-hour-stratified analyses; compare stratification cost across income settings.
