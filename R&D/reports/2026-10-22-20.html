同樣是抗生素失效：兒童抗藥性死亡減逾半、老年人增逾八成

When Drug Failure Splits by Age: AMR Deaths Halved in Children, Up 80% in the Elderly

2026-10-22 20:07 UTC · 996 中文字 · 8950 bytes · 8 來源 · 未評

主題 / Topic

抗藥性常被畫成單一上升曲線，但 GRAM 的 2024 年 Lancet 分析顯示年齡兩端方向相反：1990–2021 年 5 歲以下抗藥性歸因死亡減逾半，70 歲以上增逾八成。本報告問「同樣是抗生素失效，為何兩端走勢相反」，並檢視 3,900 萬宗死亡的資源含意。

AMR is usually drawn as one rising curve, yet the 2024 GRAM analysis moves in opposite directions at both ends of the age range: attributable AMR deaths under five fell by more than half from 1990 to 2021, while deaths at 70 and over rose over 80%. Why does drug failure diverge for these two groups, and what does 39 million cumulative deaths before 2050 mean for resource allocation?

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【事實】規模：2021 年 471 萬宗死亡與細菌抗藥性相關，其中 114 萬宗直接歸因；預測 2050 年歸因死亡 191 萬（+67.5%），2025–2050 累計 3,900 萬宗。
  • 【機制】年齡轉移：兒童端是分母效應壓過分子效應：疫苗與衛生令感染總數急降，儘管感染更易耐藥，5 歲以下歸因死亡仍由 488,000 降至 193,000，耐藥肺炎球菌由 158,000 降至 35,100；老年端兩效應同向乘大：共病推高感染數，醫院暴露與用藥強度推高耐藥比例。同期 MRSA 歸因死亡由 57,200 增至 130,000。
  • 【機制】新藥槓桿弱：WHO 管線分析指 97 個在研抗生素中 57 個屬傳統，56%（32 個）針對最高風險病原體，其中僅 12 個具創新性、4 個對關鍵級病原體有活性；2017 年 7 月以來獲批 13 個，僅 2 個屬新化學類別。
  • 【機制】排除替代解釋：GRAM 已按人口標準化，兒童端跌幅並非人口分母造成；2019 至 2021 年跌幅可由疫情社交距離解釋，但只覆蓋一年。
  • 【敏感度】：更好護理與抗藥藥可及性可避免 9,200 萬宗（區間 8,280–10,200 萬），新開發革蘭氏陰性菌抗生素至多避免 1,100 萬宗（約 8.3 倍）；獸醫端 2040 年用量基準 14.3 萬噸（較 2019 年增 29.5%），替代情境區間 +14.2% 至 −56.8%。結論在「護理與可及性優先」範圍內成立；若新藥交付速度假設大幅上調，比例會翻正。
  • Scale: 4.71 million deaths associated with bacterial AMR in 2021, 1.14 million attributable; 1.91 million attributable deaths projected for 2050 (+67.5%), 39 million cumulative over 2025–2050.
  • Mechanism (age shift): in children the denominator effect outweighs the numerator effect — vaccines and sanitation cut infections faster than resistance rose, so under-five attributable deaths fell from 488,000 to 193,000 and drug-resistant pneumococcal deaths from 158,000 to 35,100; in the elderly both effects multiply, and MRSA deaths rose from 57,200 to 130,000.
  • Mechanism (weak drug lever): of 97 antibiotics in development 57 are traditional, 56% (32) target highest-risk pathogens, only 12 of those innovative and only 4 against a critical pathogen; since July 2017 thirteen approvals include just two new chemical classes.
  • Ruling out alternatives: the child-side decline is not a population artefact since GRAM standardises by population; the 2019-to-2021 drop is explainable by distancing but covers one year only.
  • Sensitivity: better care and access avert 92 million deaths (82.8–102.0 million) against at most 11 million from new Gram-negative antibiotics (8.3x); 2040 livestock use is 143,481 tonnes under business-as-usual (+29.5% vs 110,777 tonnes in 2019), with a +14.2% to −56.8% range.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 優先序：感染控制、疫苗、快速診斷與抗藥藥可及性可避免 9,200 萬宗死亡，新抗生素研發至多 1,100 萬宗。【推論】預算固定的衛生部門若投放於醫院感染防控與藥物可及性，模型內死亡避免效率可達新藥管線 8 倍。
  • 獸醫個案：丹麥有近三十年連續用量的國家級實測：豬業抗生素噸位由 1994 年峰值至 2020 年降 52%，同期產出增至 3,260 萬頭；2010 年黃卡制度按每頭每日門檻鎖定高用量豬群，高用量保育豬群用藥於 2010 年第二季至 2011 年第一季跌約 50%，高用量母豬群自 2010 年第三季起負增長至 2012 年底。【推論】若同類政策覆蓋亞太（2040 年用量 92,687 噸、佔全球 64.6%），每減 10% 用量即每年少用約 9,270 噸抗生素。
  • 環境與基建：全球約 52% 污水經處理，近半直排河道與灌溉地。污水廠調查發現進水含 1,331 種抗藥基因亞型、單廠去除率 88.4%（區間 63.2–94.2%），但高風險抗藥基因與可移動遺傳元件共現，由沙門氏菌等優先病原體攜帶。【推論】回用水城市須加設 UV 或高級氧化，否則只是把基因從液相搬進污泥。
  • 公平：南亞 2025–2050 將佔 1,180 萬宗歸因死亡；更好護理三情境可避免 3,170 萬、2,520 萬、1,870 萬宗。【推論】資源應按可避免死亡而非藥物銷量分配。
  • Priority: infection control, vaccines, rapid diagnostics and access avert 92 million deaths against at most 11 million for new antibiotic R&D — care and access beat the drug pipeline more than eightfold for a fixed budget.
  • Veterinary case: Denmark's pig-sector antimicrobial tonnage fell 52% from the 1994 peak to 2020 while output grew to 32.6 million head; the 2010 Yellow Card scheme set per-animal-per-day thresholds; high-use weaner herds then cut consumption about 50% from Q2 2010 to Q1 2011, and high-use sow herds stayed on negative growth to end-2012.
  • Environment: about 52% of wastewater is treated globally, leaving nearly half discharged to rivers and irrigation land; a treatment-plant survey found 1,331 ARG subtypes in influent and average removal of 88.4% (63.2–94.2%), yet high-risk ARGs co-occur with mobile genetic elements in WHO priority pathogens.
  • Equity: South Asia is projected to account for 11.8 million attributable deaths over 2025–2050, with 31.7 million, 25.2 million and 18.7 million deaths avertable under three better-care scenarios.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：GRAM 屬同行評審的疾病負擔模型，屬推算而非觀測；2022 年版報告 2019 年 495 萬相關／127 萬歸因，2024 年版改以 2021 年 471 萬／114 萬為基準，口徑仍在移動。爭議點：媒體常引用 822 萬等大數，忽略「相關」與「歸因」是不同反事實。缺口：未取得 WHONET 一手數據與丹麥黃卡前後抗藥性率對照數字。下一步：追蹤 WHO 全球抗藥性監測（GLASS）報告。

Confidence: GRAM is a peer-reviewed disease-burden model, so its outputs are projections rather than observation, and the baseline is still moving (2022 edition: 4.95 million associated and 1.27 million attributable deaths for 2019; 2024 edition: 4.71 million and 1.14 million for 2021). Contested point: coverage often quotes the 8.22 million figure without noting that associated and attributable deaths rest on different counterfactuals. Gaps: no first-hand WHONET data or matched pre-/post-Yellow-Card resistance rates. Next: track the WHO GLASS report.
