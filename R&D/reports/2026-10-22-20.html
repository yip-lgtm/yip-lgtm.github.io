同樣是抗生素失效：兒童抗藥性死亡 30 年腰斬、老年人近倍增

When Drug Failure Splits by Age: AMR Deaths Halved in Children, Nearly Doubled in the Elderly

2026-10-22 20:07 UTC · 1029 中文字 · 9479 bytes · 8 來源 · 未評

主題 / Topic

抗生素抗藥性常被當成單一上升曲線，但 GRAM 在 The Lancet 的 2024 年分析顯示年齡兩端方向相反：1990–2021 年 5 歲以下兒童抗藥性死亡降近六成，70 歲以上增近九成。本報告回答「同樣是抗生素失效，為何兩端走勢相反」，並檢視 2050 年前累計 3,900 萬宗死亡預測對資源分配的直接含意。

Antimicrobial resistance is usually drawn as one rising curve, yet the 2024 GRAM analysis in The Lancet shows opposite directions at both ends of the age range: attributable deaths in children under five fell by nearly 60% between 1990 and 2021, while deaths in adults aged 70 and over rose by nearly 90%. This report asks why drug failure moves in opposite directions for these groups, and what the forecast of 39 million cumulative deaths before 2050 means for resource allocation.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【事實】規模：2021 年 471 萬宗死亡與細菌抗藥性相關，其中 114 萬宗直接歸因於抗藥性；預測 2050 年年度歸因死亡 191 萬（+67.5%），2025–2050 累計 3,900 萬宗。
  • 【機制】年齡轉移：兒童端是分母效應壓過分子效應——疫苗與衛生令感染總數急降，儘管單宗感染更可能耐藥（耐藥肺炎球菌死亡 1990 年 158,000 降至 2021 年 35,100）；老年端兩效應同向乘大：老化與共病推高感染數，醫院暴露與用藥強度推高耐藥比例。
  • 【機制】排除替代解釋：GRAM 已按人口標準化，兒童端下降並非人口分母造成；2019 至 2021 年的跌幅可由疫情社交距離解釋，但那只覆蓋單一年度。
  • 【機制】新藥槓桿弱：WHO 管線分析指 57 個傳統抗生素中僅 32 個（56%）針對最高風險優先病原體，其中僅 4 個對關鍵級病原體具活性；2017 年 7 月以來僅 2 個新化學類別獲批。
  • 【敏感度】：同一模型的兩個反事實——改善感染護理與抗藥藥可及性可避免 9,200 萬宗死亡，新開發革蘭氏陰性菌抗生素僅 1,108 萬宗（約 8.3 倍）；獸醫端 2040 年用量基準 14.3 萬噸，替代情境區間 +14.2% 至 −56.8%。結論在「護理與可及性優先」範圍內成立；若新藥交付假設大幅上調，比例會翻正。
  • Scale: GRAM counts 4.71 million AMR-associated deaths in 2021, 1.14 million attributable; attributable deaths are projected at 1.91 million by 2050 (+67.5%), 39 million cumulative over 2025–2050.
  • Mechanism (age shift): on the child side the denominator effect outweighs the numerator effect — vaccines and sanitation cut infections faster than resistance prevalence rose (drug-resistant pneumococcal deaths fell from 158,000 in 1990 to 35,100 in 2021). On the older side both effects multiply: ageing and comorbidity raise infection counts, while hospital exposure and antibiotic intensity raise the resistant share.
  • Ruling out alternatives: the child-side decline is not a population artefact, since GRAM standardises by population; the 2019-to-2021 drop is explainable by pandemic distancing, but covers one year only.
  • Mechanism (weak drug lever): only 32 of 57 traditional antibiotics (56%) target the highest-risk priority pathogens, four against critical pathogens, and two new chemical classes approved since July 2017. Carbapenem-resistant Gram-negative deaths rose from 127,000 to 216,000 and MRSA from 57,200 to 130,000.
  • Sensitivity: better infection care and access avert 92 million deaths while new Gram-negative antibiotics avert 11.08 million (8.3x); 2040 livestock use is 143,481 tonnes under business-as-usual, with a +14.2% to −56.8% alternative range. The conclusion holds within a "care and access first" range and flips if new-drug delivery assumptions rise sharply.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 優先序：感染控制、疫苗、快速診斷與抗藥藥可及性可避免 9,200 萬宗死亡，新抗生素研發只避免 1,108 萬宗。【推論】預算固定的衛生部門若投放於醫院感染防控與藥物可及性，模型內死亡避免效率可達新藥管線 8 倍以上。
  • 獸醫個案：丹麥有近三十年連續用量的國家級實測——豬業抗生素噸位由 1994 年峰值至 2020 年降 52%（2020 年 75.9 噸），同期豬隻產出增至 3,260 萬頭，指示性大腸桿菌完全敏感比例維持約 46%；2010 年黃卡制度按每頭每日門檻鎖定高用量豬群，發卡後用量數月內跌約 50%。【推論】若同類政策覆蓋 2040 年用量最大的亞洲（9.27 萬噸、佔全球 64.6%），每減 10% 用量即每年少用約 9,270 噸抗生素。
  • 環境與基建：全球僅約 52% 人口污水進廠處理；涵蓋 23 國 269 座污水廠的調查顯示抗藥基因總量平均去除 88.4%（最高 94.2%），但 57.6% 的高完整度菌株攜帶跨物種可移動抗藥基因。【推論】回用水城市須加設 UV 或高級氧化，否則只是把基因從液相搬進污泥。
  • 公平：南亞 2025–2050 將佔 1,180 萬宗歸因死亡，而更好護理情境下南亞可避免 3,170 萬宗。【推論】資源應按「可避免死亡」而非藥物銷量分配。
  • Priority: infection control, vaccines, rapid diagnostics and access avert 92 million deaths against 11.08 million for new antibiotic R&D — for a fixed health budget, the modelled efficiency of care and access is more than eight times that of the drug pipeline.
  • Veterinary case: Denmark's pig-sector antimicrobial tonnage fell 52% from the 1994 peak to 2020 (75.9 tonnes) while output grew to 32.6 million head and fully sensitive indicator E. coli held near 46%; the 2010 Yellow Card scheme set herd thresholds and the highest-use herds cut consumption by roughly half within months.
  • Environment: only about 52% of the world's population sends sewage to treatment; across 269 plants in 23 countries resistance-gene removal averaged 88.4% (up to 94.2%), yet 57.6% of high-quality assemblies carried multi-species mobile genes — reclaimed-water cities need UV or advanced oxidation.
  • Equity: South Asia is projected to account for 11.8 million attributable deaths over 2025–2050, with 31.7 million avertable under better care, so resources should follow avoidable deaths rather than drug sales.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：GRAM 屬同行評審的疾病負擔模型，是推算而非直接觀測；2024 版把 2019 年歸因死亡修訂為約 120 萬，與 2022 年版 127 萬有差，顯示口徑仍在移動。爭議點：媒體常引用 822 萬等大數，忽略「相關」與「歸因」是兩個不同反事實。缺口：未取得 WHONET 一手監測數據，亦未取得丹麥黃卡觸發前後的抗藥性對照數字。下一步：追蹤 WHO 全球抗藥性監測（GLASS）報告與 2024 優先病原體清單對應的研發資金流。

Confidence: GRAM is a peer-reviewed disease-burden model, so its outputs are projections rather than direct observation, and the 2024 revision restated 2019 attributable deaths at about 1.2 million against 1.27 million in the 2022 edition. Contested point: coverage often quotes the 8.22 million figure without noting that associated and attributable deaths rest on different counterfactuals. Gaps: no first-hand WHONET data and no matched pre- and post-Yellow-Card resistance numbers for Denmark. Next: track the WHO GLASS report and funding tied to the 2024 priority pathogens list.
