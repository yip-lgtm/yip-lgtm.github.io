主題 / Topic

  • 問題：醫院廣泛部署的專有敗血症 AI 模型，真能改善病人結果嗎？
  • 2026 年四家美國醫療系統前瞻驗證：新版模型準確率改善，但陽性預測值仍低。
  • English: Do widely deployed proprietary sepsis AI models improve outcomes? A 2026 four-system prospective validation found better discrimination but low positive predictive value.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【事實】Epic Sepsis Model v2 在四家美國醫療系統前瞻驗證：就診層 AUROC 0.82–0.92，12 小時預測層 0.75–0.85；對照臨床醫生辨識後降為 0.80–0.90。

English: ESM v2 reached encounter-level AUROC 0.82–0.92 across four US health systems; against clinician recognition it fell to 0.80–0.90.

  • 【事實】陽性預測值僅 0.13–0.26；住院層每評估 4–8 次得一例，4 小時窗口 NNE 高達 24–69。

English: Positive predictive value stayed low at 0.13–0.26, with 4–8 evaluations per true hospitalization and NNE of 24–69 at the 4-hour horizon.

  • 【機制】原版以抗生素作預測因子、用非臨床結果標籤驗證且泛化有限；2022 年新版改用梯度提升樹加各院本地微調，修正部分成因但仍受病種比例影響。

English: The original model used antibiotics as predictors, non-clinical outcome labels, and limited generalizability; the 2022 revision added gradient-boosted trees and local fine-tuning but remains sensitive to case mix.

  • 【替代解釋】高 AUROC 不等於臨床可用，因為 AUROC 對患病率不敏感；真正成本在 PPV 與警報量，模型未必失效，可能只是驗證標籤與臨床定義不一致。

English: High AUROC does not equal clinical utility because AUROC is insensitive to prevalence; the real cost is PPV and alert volume, and the gap may reflect mismatched labels rather than a broken model.

  • 【敏感度】改用 12 小時窗口則 NNE 21–35、PPV 0.03–0.05；8 小時靜默策略雖大減警報量，低 PPV 與高 NNE 依舊，結論只在「人手即時覆核」前提下成立。

English: A 12-hour horizon gives NNE 21–35 and PPV 0.03–0.05; an 8-hour silencing strategy cuts volume but leaves low PPV and high NNE, so conclusions hold only if alerts are reviewed immediately.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【事實】2020 年 4 月密歇根大學因護理人員反映過度警報而暫停敗血症警報；24 家醫院研究顯示首波疫情期間每日警報增 43%，同期住院人數反減 35%。

English: In April 2020 the University of Michigan paused sepsis alerts after nursing reports of over-alerting; across 24 hospitals daily alerts rose 43% while census fell 35%.

  • 【事實】截至 2025 年 8 月，95 個醫療組織（731 間醫院）已用新版模型，逾 100 個組織仍用原版；原版外部驗證曾漏掉 67% 敗血症病人，卻對 18% 住院者發警報。

English: By August 2025, 95 organizations (731 hospitals) used the revised model while over 100 still ran the original, which external validation found missed 67% of sepsis patients yet alerted on 18% of admissions.

  • 【推論】延遲抗生素每小時校正死亡 OR 1.09（95% CI 1.05–1.13），故警報價值在於轉化成及早治療；但 PPV 0.13 表示多數警報無法行動化。

English: Each hour of antibiotic delay carried an adjusted mortality OR of 1.09 (95% CI 1.05–1.13), so alerts help only if converted into treatment; PPV 0.13 means most cannot be acted on.

  • 【推論】CMS 把 SEP-1 納入醫院價值採購，但 2025 年系統評估（17 項觀察研究）未找到中至高等級證據支持其降低敗血症死亡；複雜臨床表現正是合規與死亡的混因子。

English: CMS ties SEP-1 to value-based purchasing, yet a 2025 review of 17 observational studies found no moderate- or high-level evidence of lower mortality; clinical complexity confounds the compliance link.

  • 【推論】可稽核做法：以 NNE 4–69 量度各院 PPV，未達標者先補強臨床流程，不以 AI 警報替代判斷；同廠 DTI 模型偏差曾對原住民高 14%，亦須分層驗證。

English: Measure local PPV against an NNE of 4–69; where unmet, strengthen clinical workflow rather than substitute AI alarms for judgment, and validate by subgroup as a sibling DTI model was 14% worse for Indigenous patients.
