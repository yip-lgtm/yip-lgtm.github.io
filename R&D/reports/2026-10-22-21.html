主題 / Topic

鈣鈦礦—矽堆疊太陽能要在成本上追平矽基，模組究竟要可靠多久？本報告結合同儕審評 LCOE 敏感度、IEA PVPS 劣化機制與 Oxford PV 廠方文件，量化效率與融資年限如何決定它能否提供低成本、低碳電力。

How long must perovskite–silicon tandem modules operate before they can beat silicon on cost? Combining a peer-reviewed LCOE sensitivity analysis, the IEA PVPS degradation review and Oxford PV's own warranty documents, this report quantifies how module efficiency and financing tenor jointly determine whether tandem can deliver low-cost, low-carbon electricity.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 效率門檻 — Gao 等（2026）以 1,500 全載小時、25 年壽命為矽基基準，LCOE 約 0.157 CNY/kWh；堆疊效率 29% 時須運轉逾 21 年才低於該基準，32% 降至 19 年，35% 需 18 年。堆疊變數 BOS 成本佔比亦由 57% 降至 51%。
  • 【機制】 — IEA PVPS T13-30:2025 指出：混鹵相分離（Br 逾 20% 易分離）、可動離子濃度比矽高數個數量級、85°C 光暗循環熱分解、軟弱易損材料，叠加使大面積模組戶外壽命仍不確定，實驗室壽命報告分歧大；效率越高，製程與封裝越難同時穩定。
  • 【敏感度】 — 依 Gao 的資本回收公式重算：可融資年限由 25 年縮至 10 年，資本回收項放大 1.74 倍（折現 6%）、1.59 倍（8%）、1.48 倍（10%）；20 年保固於 8% 仍為 1.09 倍。此處只計年限效應，不含殘值、劣化與 O&M。效率升至 32% 時門檻降至 19 年，結論即翻正。
  • 保固落差 — Oxford PV 官網稱現行模組「10+ Years lifetime warranty」，Centaur 2LT 資料表列 25% 效率、540 W、10 年壽命。pv Europe 報 Gen 3「Centaur 3」2026 年推出、550 W、26% 效率、15 年保固，2027 年目標 20 年；PES Europe 則稱 15 年屬 Gen 3 原型路線圖，30 年為本十年末目標。
  • 現場證據不足 — Brandenburg 廠矽底板名目產能約 100 MW，堆疊線僅「數十 MW」且實際開兩班，部分產能供開發試作，短期難累積 25 年現場劣化樣本。
  • Efficiency threshold — Gao et al. (2026) use 1,500 full-load hours and a 25-year life for the silicon benchmark, LCOE ≈ 0.157 CNY/kWh. A 29%-efficient tandem module must run beyond 21 years to undercut it; 32% lowers the requirement to 19 years and 35% to 18 years. Variable BOS cost share falls from 57% (silicon) to 51% (tandem).
  • 【機制/Mechanism】 — IEA PVPS T13-30:2025 attributes the uncertainty to mixed-halide phase segregation (above ~20% Br), mobile-ion concentrations orders of magnitude above silicon, thermal decomposition across an 85 °C light/dark cycle, and mechanically weak films. On large-area modules these leave field lifetime unsettled and laboratory lifetimes widely dispersed: higher efficiency makes processing and packaging harder to stabilise simultaneously.
  • 【敏感度/Sensitivity】 — Recomputing the capital recovery term from Gao's LCOE formula, shortening financeable life from 25 to 10 years multiplies it by 1.74 at 6% discount, 1.59 at 8% and 1.48 at 10%; a 20-year warranty is still 1.09× at 8%. This isolates tenor only, excluding salvage, degradation and O&M. At 32% efficiency the threshold falls to 19 years and the conclusion reverses.
  • Warranty gap — Oxford PV's own site states "10+ Years lifetime warranty for our module products today"; the Centaur 2LT datasheet lists 25% efficiency, 540 W and a 10-year lifetime. pv Europe reports the 2026 Centaur 3 (550 W, 26%) with a 15-year warranty and a 20-year target for 2027; PES Europe instead describes 15 years as a Gen 3 prototype roadmap figure, with 30 years an end-of-decade target.
  • Thin field evidence — At Brandenburg, HJT bottom-cell nameplate capacity is about 100 MW, while the tandem line is only "tens of megawatts", running two shifts with part of the line used for development campaigns — too little to accumulate 25-year field degradation samples quickly.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • Brandenburg 案例 — 產能結構顯示證據不足有具體成因：底板 100 MW、堆疊數十 MW、兩班運作，商業出貨與研發資源競爭。
  • 【推論】融資 — 10 年保固令資本回收項較 25 年基準高 1.59 倍（8% 折現）；若項目仍按 25 年售電模型估值，差額只能由殘值、維修費或產量假設吸收。NREL 估算 25% 效率、3 GW/年美國產能下，2 端子最低可持續價為 0.428 美元/Wdc。
  • 【推論】土地與公益 — Gao 估算堆疊變數 BOS 佔比 51%，低於矽基 57%；Oxford PV 目標 2035 年 35% 效率、LCOE 較純矽低約 20%。限地城市與高 BOS 地區或受益最大，前提是壽命風險不轉化為更高貸款利率。
  • 安全缺口 — IEA 要求鈣鈦礦吸收層承受約 85°C 完整光暗循環；本題未找到鉛浸出或 RoHS 豁免的可引用量化估計，亦未找到 10 年保固令 25 年項目失去可融資性的公開文件。
  • Brandenburg case — The capacity split shows why evidence is thin: 100 MW of bottom cells, tens of MW of tandem capacity, two shifts, with commercial output competing with development campaigns.
  • 【推論/Inference】Finance — A 10-year warranty raises the capital recovery term 1.59× above a 25-year baseline at 8% discount; if projects still price a 25-year offtake model, the gap must be absorbed by salvage, maintenance reserve or yield assumptions. NREL estimates a minimum sustainable price of US$0.428/Wdc for a 2-terminal design at 25% module efficiency and 3 GW/yr US production.
  • 【推論/Inference】Land and public value — Gao puts variable BOS at 51% for tandem versus 57% for silicon, and Oxford PV targets 35% module efficiency and roughly 20% lower LCOE than pure silicon by 2035; land-constrained cities and high-BOS regions would gain most, provided lifetime risk does not become a higher borrowing cost.
  • Safety gap — IEA requires the absorber to survive an ~85 °C day–night cycle. This report found no citable quantification of lead leaching or RoHS exemptions, and no public document showing that a 10-year warranty makes a 25-year project unfinanceable.
