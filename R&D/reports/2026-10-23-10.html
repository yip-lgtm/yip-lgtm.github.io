Copper Near Record Highs: Mine Failures, Negative Treatment Charges and an Acid Shortage

主題 / Topic

本報告回答：2026 年精煉銅被預測為小幅過剩，為何銅價仍逼近歷史高位，並把成本推向變壓器、電網與數據中心？範圍只限銅精礦至精煉銅供應鏈，並估算缺口對採購價的邊界。

This report asks why copper prices stay near record highs in 2026 even though refined supply is forecast to show a modest surplus, and how that reprices transformers, grids and data centres. The scope is the concentrate-to-refined chain. I quantify the price mechanism and bound the pass-through to procurement budgets, separating measured facts from inference.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】Grasberg 泥石流令 2025 年產量約為 2024 年一半；該礦佔全球礦產銅近 4%。Kamoa-Kakula 亦因淹水減產近三分之一。兩者把精煉市場推入缺口，觸發異常銅價升幅，Grasberg 全面復產要到 2028 年。
  • 【機制】年度銅精礦加工費由 2025 年每乾公噸 21.25 美元降至 2026 年零；10 月 9 日現貨為負 235 至負 240 美元。冶煉產能過剩而礦石緊缺，定價權由冶煉廠轉向礦方。
  • 【機制】中國 5 月起禁硫酸出口至年底，逾 15% 全球原生銅靠硫酸浸出。剛果（金）45% 產量依賴此法，酸占當地 SxEW 成本 20%。這是精礦之外的第二個供應閥門。
  • 【敏感度】ICSG 預測 2026 過剩 10.4 萬噸、2027 過剩 36.8 萬噸。對照 2025 年精煉產量 2900 萬噸，僅占 0.36% 與 1.27%（本人計算），不足以解釋高價；若 Grasberg 提前復產或酸禁令提早解除，過剩會翻為不足。

Mechanism: the Grasberg mudflow cut 2025 output to around half of 2024, and Grasberg is almost 4% of global mined copper. Seismic flooding cut Kamoa-Kakula by nearly one third against guidance. Together they tipped the refined market into deficit and drove exceptional copper price rises, with full Grasberg recovery not anticipated until 2028. Mechanism: the annual copper concentrate treatment charge fell from US$21.25/dmt in 2025 to zero for 2026; the 9 October spot assessment was about minus US$235 to minus US$240/dmt. Smelter overcapacity against tight concentrate moved rent from refiners to miners. Mechanism: China's May-to-year-end sulphuric acid export ban touches over 15% of primary copper made by leaching; the DRC derives 45% of its copper this way and acid is 20% of local SxEW cost. Sensitivity: ICSG forecasts a 104 kt refined surplus in 2026 and 368 kt in 2027. Against 29 Mt of 2025 refined output that is 0.36% and 1.27% (my calculation), too small to explain record prices; earlier Grasberg recovery or an earlier acid-ban reversal would flip the balance.

  • 【推論】IEA 估 2030 年數據中心擴建帶來 51.2 萬噸銅需求，最多占 2024 年總需求 2%。

IEA's Energy and AI finds insulation, copper and aluminium together account for around half of power transformer cost, with grain-oriented steel alone near 20%, so copper repricing passes directly into transformer procurement. Inference, Panama case: Cobre Panamá has been idle since late 2023, removing over 1% of global copper supply and about 4.5% of Panama GDP. Its 38 Mt stockpile holds roughly 70 kt of recoverable copper, worth about US$987 million at the 22 October LME three-month price of US$14,096/t (my calculation). Inference, Chile case: July 2026 output was 403,424 t, down 9.4% year on year, the weakest July since 2011; IEA's high-production case sees Chilean copper-mining water demand above 650 million m3 by 2035. Inference: IEA puts 2030 data-centre copper demand at 512 kt, up to 2% of 2024 demand.
