主題 / Topic

3D 列印金屬件疲勞失效，表面粗糙度與內部孔隙何者主導？HIP 與表面精加工各挽回多少壽命？低成本顯微 CT 能否普及醫療件缺陷檢測？

For additively manufactured metal parts, does surface roughness or internal porosity dominate fatigue failure? How much life do hot isostatic pressing (HIP) and surface finishing each recover? Can low-cost micro-CT make defect inspection routine for medical parts?

【Mechanism】Fatigue cracks initiate at surface or internal defects, so the two compete for life.

【Mechanism】HIP closes internal pores under high pressure and temperature and suppresses initiation, but does not improve the surface.
