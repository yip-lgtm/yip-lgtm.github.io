Title: Charging After Midnight Can Age Distribution Transformers Faster

Charging After Midnight Can Age Distribution Transformers Faster

主題 / Topic

電動車接入低壓配電網，會否加快變壓器熱老化？本報告檢視「午夜後充電」與分時收費會否反效果，並量化機制、成本與可行控制。範圍限住宅配電變壓器。

Do EVs on low-voltage distribution networks accelerate transformer thermal ageing? This report asks whether charging after midnight, or time-of-use tariffs, can backfire, and quantifies the mechanism, cost and workable control. Scope is limited to residential distribution transformers.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】 IEEE C57.91 動態熱模型：25 kVA 桿上變壓器服務 12 戶兼 6 車時，不受控交流 Level 2 充電令年等效老化 FEQA 達 9.79；佛蒙特同條件只有 2.34。
  • 【機制】 兩地行程與充電排程相同，差異源自環境溫度與繞組熱點升溫，非充電電量。熱點升溫加速絕緣紙劣化；曾短暫接近「每年老化 50 年」。
  • 【機制】 午夜後充電令 FEQA 由 9.79 升至 10.81、2.34 升至 2.91。低價窗把多車同步，令同一變壓器出現窄時段高負荷；論文指分時收費可造成同類反效果。
  • 【敏感度】 情境一：佛蒙特 2.34，溫度回饋減 4.8 倍，充電成功率逾 97%。情境二：鳳凰城 9.79，回饋減 6.3 倍，p<10⁻³。情境三：NLR 指多數公用事業平均負載低於 30%、峰值低於 60%，若餘裕充足，增量老化可不重要——結論由「有害」變「不重大」，不會翻正。
  • 【事實】 Ofgem 2026-06-11 決定：架空低壓服務單位費 0.35→1.44 千英鎊，地下 1.60→2.65 千英鎊；相關開支 ex ante 額度 26.4 億英鎊。
  • 【Mechanism】 An IEEE C57.91 dynamic thermal model: on a 25 kVA pole-mounted transformer serving 12 homes and 6 plug-in EVs, uncontrolled AC Level 2 charging drove annual factor of equivalent ageing (FEQA) to 9.79, versus 2.34 in Burlington under the same setup.
  • 【Mechanism】 Travel and charging schedules were identical in both cities, so the difference comes from ambient temperature and winding hot-spot rise, not energy charged. Hot-spot heating accelerates cellulose-insulation ageing; one uncontrolled hot-day case briefly approached 50 years of ageing per year.
  • 【Mechanism】 After-midnight charging raised FEQA from 9.79 to 10.81 and from 2.34 to 2.91. A cheap window synchronises vehicles, creating a narrow high-load spike on one transformer; the authors warn time-of-use pricing can cause the same effect.
  • 【Sensitivity】 Scenario 1: Burlington 2.34, cut 4.8x by temperature feedback, over 97% of charges reached at least 95%. Scenario 2: Phoenix 9.79, cut 6.3x, p<10^-3. Scenario 3: NLR reports utility-reported average loading below 30% and peak below 60% in many cases; with ample headroom the incremental ageing can be immaterial, so the conclusion shifts from harmful to not material, not to beneficial.
  • 【Fact】 Ofgem's 11 June 2026 decision: overhead low-voltage service unit rate £0.35k to £1.44k, underground £1.60k to £2.65k, with a £2.64bn ex-ante allowance for load-related expenditure.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】 POWER Engineers 模型（688 個住宅變壓器）：30% 滲透約 7.3%（50 個）需升級，60% 滲透 110 個（16%）；按其 5,000 萬台、3,000 萬台服務住宅的假設即 220 萬至 480 萬台。NLR 口徑 6,000–8,000 萬台，規模屬條件式。
  • 【推論】 單位費是追收上限：架空每單位約 4.1 倍，地下升 66%。UKPN 指 80A 單相 18 kVA 供電上，7 kW 充電器連同既有負荷超 18 kVA 須升級至 100A。
  • 【推論】 個案：UK Power Networks「Shift」（2019–2021）把晚高峰電動車需求平均削 79%，6–9 時中位削 82%（有獎勵）、65%（無）；住戶晚高峰削 44%，85% 節次智能充電，只需 20% 插電時間。項目明言次高峰不應成為不做智能充電的理由。
  • 【推論】 溫度或電網回饋在 30%–60% 滲透、最高 10.81 FEQA 時最值錢；純分時折扣在佛蒙特 2.34 已安全時仍推高 24%，省電費而損資產。
  • 【Inference】 POWER Engineers model (688 residential transformers): at 30% EV penetration about 7.3% (50 units) need upgrading; at 60%, 110 units (16%). On its own assumption of 50 million US transformers with 30 million serving homes, that is 2.2 to 4.8 million units. NLR's 60-80 million denominator makes the scale conditional.
  • 【Inference】 Unit rates cap customer recovery: overhead roughly 4.1x per unit, underground up 66%. UKPN notes that on an 80A single-phase 18 kVA supply, a 7 kW charger plus existing load above 18 kVA forces an upgrade to 100A.
  • 【Inference】 Case: UK Power Networks' Shift trial (2019-2021) cut evening-peak EV demand by 79% on average; median 6-9 p.m. reduction 82% with incentives, 65% without; household evening peak fell 44%, 85% of sessions smart-charged, and only 20% of at-home plugged-in time was needed. The project concluded secondary peaks should not be a reason not to smart charge.
  • 【Inference】 Grid- or temperature-aware charging pays most at 30-60% penetration and up to 10.81 FEQA per year; pure price windows still raise ageing 24% (2.34 to 2.91) in the cool case where 2.34 was already safe — saving bills while spending asset life.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：機制較高。IEEE 論文用 C57.91 熱模型、蒙地卡羅模擬及現場參數校準，但只涉一台 25 kVA、6 車、兩地氣候，外推需保留。

爭議：分時電價削電網峰值，卻可能把峰值搬進變壓器；與 UKPN「Shift」不矛盾——分散帶回饋可行，純搬移不可取。

缺口：未找到「因電動車更換變壓器」的可稽核公用事業件數；SCE 每台 6.64 萬美元單位成本與 UKPN「129 站、約 117 萬英鎊」說法均未核實，故不採用。

下一步：把變壓器熱點回饋與電價訊號結合。

Confidence: the mechanism is well supported. The IEEE paper uses a C57.91 thermal model, Monte Carlo simulation and field transformer parameters, but covers one 25 kVA unit, six vehicles and two climates, so extrapolation is limited. Dispute: time-of-use pricing can cut system peaks yet move peaks into transformers; this is consistent with UKPN's Shift trial, which relied on spread, feedback-based charging rather than a single window. Gaps: no auditable utility count of transformers replaced because of EV load was found; an SCE figure of $66,400 per transformer and a UKPN claim about 129 substations and £1.17m could not be verified and are not used. Next step: combine transformer hot-spot feedback with price signals.
