主題 / Topic

低強度爆炸訓練會否損害腦健康，關鍵可能不是單次峰值，而是累計劑量。本文比較累積衝量與神經行為症狀、INVICTA 前瞻隊列、MRI 結構病變與退伍軍人長期診斷，並檢視美軍監測為何落後。

Low-intensity blast training may harm brain health, and the decisive variable may be cumulative dose rather than any single peak overpressure. This report compares accumulated impulse against neurobehavioral symptoms, the prospective INVICTA cohort, MRI structural findings and long-term veteran diagnoses, and asks why US military monitoring lags behind the evidence.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】Military Medicine 2024，46 人（23 受訓、13 教官、10 對照）：峰值、衝量、次數皆強影響症狀，但僅「基線 NSI＋累積衝量」通過驗證，單用峰值失敗。總劑量比任一次峰值更貼合症狀變化；此為劑量學證據，非病理機制直證。

[Mechanism] In 46 personnel (23 trainees, 13 instructors, 10 controls), peak, impulse and repetition all correlated with symptoms, but only baseline NSI plus cumulative impulse survived validation; peak alone failed. Total dose tracked symptom change better than any single peak. This is dosimetric evidence, not direct pathological proof.

  • 【機制】INVICTA 前瞻 224 人、5 年，炮手暴露 >4.0 psi：數分鐘內短時記憶、EEG 複雜度、tau、UCHL1 改變；逾 20% 受曝者聽力門檻顯著變化；教官約兩年 12 次訓練者記憶與步態變化更大、更久，支持反覆暴露延遲恢復。

[Mechanism] In the 224-person, five-year INVICTA cohort, gunners exposed above 4.0 psi showed short-term memory, EEG complexity, tau and UCHL1 changes within minutes; over 20% of exposed personnel showed significant hearing-threshold shifts. Instructors with roughly 12 exposures over two years had larger and longer-lasting memory and gait changes, consistent with delayed recovery after repeated exposure.

  • 【量化】Radiology 2026，564 人 MRI：白質高訊號 215/564（38.1%）與累積暴露無關（P=.82）；動脈瘤 33/564（5.9%），高曝組 21/220 對低曝組 6/220，OR 3.76（1.49–9.52），多變量 OR 1.81。「爆炸必然造成廣泛白質病變」不成立。

[Quantification] MRI of 564 personnel found white-matter hyperintensities in 215/564 (38.1%), unrelated to cumulative exposure (P=.82); aneurysms appeared in 33/564 (5.9%), with 21/220 in the high-exposure group versus 6/220 in the low group (OR 3.76, 1.49–9.52; multivariate OR 1.81). The blanket claim that blasts cause widespread white-matter damage does not hold.

  • 【替代解釋】Neurology 2026，36,641 名退伍軍人：自報爆炸傷與癲癇 APR 1.79（1.47–2.18）、中風 1.35、神經病 1.38；但低強度高風險職業反而肝 C 0.67、糖尿病 0.84，加成次可加，較可能反映健康挑選與篩查差異。

[Alternative explanation] Among 36,641 veterans, self-reported blast injury was associated with epilepsy (APR 1.79, 1.47–2.18), stroke (1.35) and neuropathy (1.38). Yet low-intensity high-risk occupations showed lower hepatitis C (0.67) and diabetes (0.84), and the effect is not additive, pointing to health-selection and screening differences.

  • 【敏感度】情境一：GBEV 中位切分 OR 3.76，多變量調整後 1.81，方向不變但幅度減半；改用逐次衝量仍無白質證據。情境二：INVICTA 的 GFAP、血管反應性與 7 項 ANAM 測驗無變化，終點若屬慢性結構病則急性標記不可外推。結論只在反覆 >4 psi 與急性效應範圍內成立。

[Sensitivity] Scenario one: the median-split GBEV gives OR 3.76, falling to 1.81 after multivariate adjustment; direction holds but magnitude halves, and per-event impulse still yields no white-matter evidence. Scenario two: GFAP, vascular reactivity and seven ANAM measures showed no change, so acute markers cannot be extrapolated to chronic structural disease. Conclusions hold only for repeated exposure above 4 psi and acute effects.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】138,488 名高風險人員僅 119,479（86%）完成基線，GAO 門檻 95%，缺口約 19,009 人；陸軍 87%、海軍 59%、空軍 81%，另 198,161 名新兵在入訓站已評估。延後發現的代價轉嫁退伍後：癲癇、中風、神經病患病比上升。

[Inference] Only 119,479 of 138,488 high-risk personnel (86%) completed baseline assessment against a 95% GAO threshold, leaving roughly 19,009 people uncovered: Army 87%, Navy 59%, Air Force 81%, while 198,161 recruits were already screened at accession stations. The cost of delayed detection shifts to post-service, where epilepsy, stroke and neuropathy prevalence ratios rise.

  • 【具體案例】GAO 訪 Bragg、Sill、Quantico、Hurlburt Field 四處、73 人（不可外推）；高危崗位含機槍手、炮兵、突擊兵、排爆、狙擊手；海軍工業衛生人員 50–60% 工時處理此危害；陸軍部分靶場把實手榴彈由每次 2 枚減至 1 枚。

[Case study] GAO interviewed 73 people across Bragg, Sill, Quantico and Hurlburt Field (not generalizable). High-risk roles include machine gunners, artillery, assault engineers, explosive ordnance disposal and snipers; Navy industrial hygiene staff spend 50–60% of working hours on this hazard, and some Army ranges cut live grenades from two per round to one.

  • 【推論】縱向追蹤僅 100 名海軍特種作戰人員（2029-03 完成）；職業暴露上限研究 2027-01、感測器研究 2029-01。若資源評估不納入逐次衝量紀錄，2026-12-31 到期的兩項 GAO 建議或只提升評估率，未必改善劑量知識。

[Inference] Longitudinal follow-up covers only 100 Navy special operations personnel, completing March 2029; occupational exposure-limit work lands January 2027 and sensor research January 2029. If resource assessments do not capture per-event impulse, the two GAO recommendations due 31 December 2026 may raise assessment rates without improving dose knowledge.

  • 【敏感度】4 psi 只是暫定安全指引，尚無職業限值；只按峰值設限會漏掉多次中低強度暴露，改記累積衝量則執行成本未知。

[Sensitivity] The 4 psi figure is a provisional safety guideline, not an occupational limit. Limiting by peak alone misses repeated moderate exposures, while recording cumulative impulse has unknown implementation cost.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：對「反覆亞震蕩爆炸可致短暫神經行為與生物標記改變、反覆暴露延長恢復」為中至高；對慢性結構與臨床病果為低至中，因 MRI 與退伍軍人研究屬觀察性，暴露多為自報或代理量。

Confidence is moderate-to-high that repeated low-level blast exposure produces short-term neurobehavioral and biomarker changes and delays recovery, but only low-to-moderate for chronic structural and clinical outcomes, since the MRI and veteran studies are observational and exposure is largely self-reported or proxied.

未解缺口：GAO 無年度暴露人數、無金額，故成本無法估算；澳洲 DVA 2026 報告與 NATO STO 指南全文遭 403，未採用其數字；4 psi 尚非職業限值。

Unresolved gaps: the GAO report gives no annual exposed headcount and no dollar figures, so costs cannot be estimated; the 2026 Australian DVA report and the NATO STO guideline were blocked (403), so their numbers were not used; and 4 psi remains a guideline rather than an occupational limit.
