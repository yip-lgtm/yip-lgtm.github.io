主題 / Topic

美軍穿甲彈由鈾合金轉鎢合金，真係更平、更安全？本題量化比較設施成本、十年生命週期與供應集中度，並檢視健康外部性有無計入。大口徑與小口徑結論相反。

This report asks whether replacing depleted uranium with tungsten alloy in military penetrators is genuinely cheaper and safer. It compares facility cost, ten-year life-cycle drivers and supply concentration, and checks whether health externalities are priced in.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【機制】成本差異來自工藝與退場處理。ATAC、COMVAT、KEM 平和期設施成本由全鈾 50 萬美元到全鎢 575 萬美元，因兩者產線不能互換，固定成本須按口徑與批量攤分。

Cost differences come from process and demilitarization. Peacetime facility costs for ATAC, COMVAT and KEM range from USD 0.5 million for all-depleted-uranium lines to USD 5.75 million for all-tungsten lines, because the two lines are not interchangeable.

  • 【機制】十年粗估差異由四項驅動構成：鎢需額外研發（ATAC）、囤積利鈾、COMVAT 製造利鎢、去mil化利鎢；「鈾一定較平」不成立。

A ten-year rough-order-of-magnitude life-cycle comparison separates four drivers: extra tungsten R&D at ATAC, stockpile additions favouring uranium, a manufacturing differential favouring tungsten in COMVAT, and demilitarization favouring tungsten.

  • 大口徑時兩者成本大致相等；小口徑如海軍 20mm Phalanx，鎢彈反而較平。

At large caliber the two are roughly cost-equivalent; at small caliber, such as the Navy 20 mm Phalanx, tungsten is cheaper.

  • 替代解釋排除：供應風險不只源自出口政策。USGS 指中國長期佔全球鎢礦產逾九成，美國無顯著境內鎢礦，靠進口與國防儲備，集中度屬結構性問題。

USGS shows China has supplied over 90 percent of world tungsten mine output for years and the United States has no significant domestic mine output, relying on imports and the defense stockpile.

  • 【敏感度】若鎢價上升或出口審批收緊，全鎢在大口徑由「相等」翻為較貴。若完整計入清理、醫療監測與退役廢料成本，鈾的優勢消失或反轉。結論只適用於未計外部成本的比較。

If tungsten prices rise or export approvals tighten, the all-tungsten option at large caliber flips from parity to more expensive; if cleanup, monitoring and retired-waste costs are internalized, uranium's advantage disappears or reverses.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【推論】以 50 萬對 575 萬美元設施差額計，全鎢改產約為全鈾的 11.5 倍。若單批量不足攤薄固定成本，單位彈藥成本上升；量產後則相反。

On a USD 0.5 million versus USD 5.75 million facility gap, an all-tungsten conversion costs about 11.5 times the all-uranium baseline; unit cost rises if one buy cannot amortize fixed cost.

  • 具體案例：海軍 20mm Phalanx 小口徑系統，研究明言鎢彈較平，同時警告相關結論須先完成兩種材料的特性化驗證。

In the Navy 20 mm Phalanx case the study states tungsten penetrators are less costly, while cautioning that conclusions require thorough characterization of both materials.

  • 健康面向：鈾作業帶來低階廢料處置負擔與環境關注；DOE Fernald 產能缺口意味需求短期上升可能延長污染場址治理。未找到可引用的每發彈藥健康成本，故不推算損失。

Uranium work carries low-level waste disposal burdens; DOE Fernald capacity shortfalls could extend cleanup at existing contaminated sites. No auditable per-round health cost was found, so no loss is extrapolated.
