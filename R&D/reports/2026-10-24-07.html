AI 編碼工具到底有冇加快開發者？兩份隨機對照實驗的相反答案

Do AI Coding Tools Actually Speed Up Developers? Two RCTs, Opposite Answers

2026-10-24 07:08 UTC · 912 中文字 · 8512 bytes · 4 來源 · 通過品質閘

主題 / Topic

AI 編碼工具究竟有冇令開發者更快？兩個最大規模的隨機對照實驗答案相反：METR 任務級試驗（16 名開發者、246 項任務，允許用 AI 時完成時間反慢 19%）與 Cui 等人在微軟、埃森哲及匿名《財富》百強企業的三場現場實驗（4,867 名開發者，完成任務數增 26.08%）。本報告比較兩者，追問差距來自模型能力、任務情境，還是量度方法。

Do AI coding tools speed up developers? Two large randomized field experiments disagree: METR's task-level trial (16 developers, 246 tasks; 19% slower with AI) versus Cui et al.'s three field experiments at Microsoft, Accenture and an anonymous Fortune 100 firm (4,867 developers; completed tasks up 26.08%). We ask whether the gap is model capability, task setting, or measurement.

核心發現 / Key Findings

  • 【事實】 METR 隨機對照試驗：16 名有經驗的開源開發者、246 項任務，允許用 AI 時完成時間延長 19%（95% CI +2% 至 +39%）。開發者事前預期省 24% 時間，事後自估省 20%。

EN: METR RCT: 16 experienced open-source developers, 246 tasks; allowing AI lengthened completion time by 19% (95% CI +2% to +39%). Developers forecast a 24% saving, later estimated 20%.

  • 【事實】 2026 年 2 月 METR 改用較新 agentic 工具重做（57 名開發者、143 個倉庫、800+ 項任務）：原班人馬加速 -18%（CI -38% 至 +9%），新招者 -4%（CI -15% 至 +9%），兩區間都含零。同期 30–50% 開發者刻意不提交「無 AI 就不想做」的任務。

EN: METR's February 2026 re-run with newer agentic tools (57 developers, 143 repos, 800+ tasks): speedup -18% for the original subset (CI -38% to +9%), -4% for new recruits (CI -15% to +9%); both intervals include zero. Meanwhile 30-50% of developers withheld tasks they would not do without AI.

  • 【事實】 Cui 等人的三場現場實驗 2026-02-27 刊登於《Management Science》：4,867 名開發者，完成任務數增 26.08%（標準誤 10.3%）。作者稱經驗較少者採用率與收益都較高，並自述每場實驗都很噪。

EN: Cui et al.'s three field experiments appeared in Management Science on 2026-02-27: 4,867 developers, completed tasks up 26.08% (SE 10.3%). The authors report less experienced developers gained more, and call each experiment noisy.

  • 【機制】 生成快不等於任務快：開發者只接受少於 44% 的 AI 產出，多數要大改，審閱與清理佔 9% 時間；倉庫平均 10 年、逾 110 萬行程式碼，AI 缺的是隱性脈絡。瓶頸在驗證，不在生成。

EN: Fast generation is not fast tasks: developers accepted under 44% of AI outputs, most needed heavy cleanup, and review plus cleanup took 9% of time; repos average 10 years and 1.1M+ lines, so AI lacks tacit context. The bottleneck is verification, not generation.

  • 【替代解釋】 若主因只是模型不夠強，換上 2025 年底 agentic 工具後負效應應持續，但估計已由 -19% 移向 0。兩研究量度對象不同——METR 量每項任務耗時，Cui 等人量已完成任務數，可同時成立。

EN: Ruled-out alternative: if weak models were the only cause, the negative effect should persist with late-2025 agentic tools, yet the estimate moved from -19% toward zero. The studies measure time per task versus tasks completed, so both can hold at once.

  • 【敏感度】 METR 兩輪區間（+2%…+39%、-38%…+9%）都覆蓋「無可檢出差異」，故「AI 明顯拖慢資深開發者」只在窄區間成立；Cui 等人的 26.08% 標準誤 10.3%、跨實驗差異大，增益只在特定任務型態下成立。

EN: Sensitivity: both METR intervals (+2%…+39% and -38%…+9%) cover "no detectable difference", so "AI clearly slows seniors" holds only narrowly; Cui et al.'s 26.08% carries SE 10.3% and large cross-experiment variation, so the gain holds only for particular task mixes.

潛在影響 / Potential Impact

  • 【事實】 證據基礎只有兩個樣本：企業端 4,867 名開發者，與 METR 的 57 名開發者、800+ 項任務。因任務型態不同，「+26%」與「無可檢出差異」並不矛盾。

EN: The evidence base is two samples: 4,867 developers in enterprises and METR's 57 developers over 800+ tasks. Different task mixes mean a 26% gain and a null result are not contradictory.

  • 【推論】 採購 ROI：若照 26.08% 任務增益入帳，而實際是資深開發者維護十年級倉庫，偏離最多可達 26 個百分點；METR 的無可檢出加速宜作下界。

EN: Procurement ROI: booking a 26.08% task-count gain while the real setting is senior developers on decade-old code can overstate by up to 26 percentage points; METR's undetectable gain is a safer lower bound.

  • 【推論】 具體案例：微軟與埃森哲在日常業務中隨機授權 Copilot（埃森哲按個人，匿名百強把 432 個團隊分 6 週分批開放）。同類授權擴展到維護成熟倉庫的資深團隊，最可能出現任務數持平而缺陷修補週期延長。

EN: Concrete case: Microsoft and Accenture randomized Copilot access inside normal business (individual at Accenture; 432 teams at the Fortune 100 firm released over 6 weeks). Extending that to senior teams on mature codebases would most plausibly hold task counts flat while lengthening defect-fix cycles.

  • 【推論】 勞動市場與政策：開發者預期（-24%）與實測（+19%）之間約 43 個百分點落差，代表以「用 AI 即有 20% 增益」為前提的培訓、績效考核或人力決策會系統性誤判資深工程師工作量。

EN: Labour market and policy: a ~43-point gap between developers' forecasts (-24%) and measured results (+19%) means training, performance review or headcount decisions premised on a 20% AI gain will misjudge senior engineers' workload.

簡評 / Commentary

可信度：METR 兩輪皆隨機但樣本細（16 與 57 人），2026 年一輪區間已含零，作者稱為「非常弱的證據」並認為很可能是下限——屬自我糾正，應作披露而非結論。Cui 等人樣本大得多，但作者自述跨實驗差異大，且全文受出版商封鎖，本報告只引用摘要可核對數字，未能核實分企業效果與其信賴區間。缺口：兩份研究的任務型態不可直接換算；本題未找到同一批開發者、同樣任務下 AI 與非 AI 的可比成本數據。數字屬 2026 年 2 月快照。

Confidence: both METR rounds are randomized but small, the 2026 interval spans zero, and METR calls it very weak evidence and probably a lower bound — disclosure, not a conclusion. Cui et al. has far more participants but the authors call each experiment noisy, and the full text is paywalled, so only abstract-verifiable numbers are used; per-company effects and their intervals remain unverified. Gap: task mixes are not convertible between studies, and no comparable cost-per-task data was found for the same developers on the same tasks. Figures are a February 2026 snapshot.
