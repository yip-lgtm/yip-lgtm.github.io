黑閃

Cross butterfly step, then slip and hook. Solo shadow only.

READY

05:10

交叉蝴蝶步 · 閃身勾拳

Light feet. Cross off the line. Slip the head. Hook.

WHAT

交叉蝴蝶步加閃身勾拳

蝴蝶步輕腳跳。後腳快速跨過前腳，離開對手直線。頭閃開中線，同邊即時勾。唔好踢步，唔好兩手離開臉。
Butterfly bounce. Rear foot crosses the lead to leave the power line. Head slips off centre. Hook on the same side. No kicking. Hands stay up.

HOW

  1. 輕腳蝴蝶跳。身輕，頭唔晃。
    Light butterfly bounce. Soft body. Head still.
  2. 交叉步。後腳跨過前腳外側，踏實。
    Cross step. Rear foot crosses outside the lead. Plant.
  3. 閃身。頭往外側輕倾，離開中線。
    Slip. Head tips outside. Leave the centre line.
  4. 勾拳。閃完同邊手勾，打完即收。
    Hook on the slip side. Snap home.
  5. 換邊再做。交叉 → 閃 → 勾，一口氣做完。
    Swap sides. Cross, slip, hook. One breath.

RULE

單打空拳。空地。唔好近牆。交叉步慢做先穩，再加快。暈就停。
Solo shadow. Open floor. Slow the cross step until it is stable, then speed up. Stop if dizzy.

呢個不代替今日洛馬 15 + A–E。練完再做，或恢復日做。
Does not replace today's Loma 15 + A–E. After the session, or on recovery day.
