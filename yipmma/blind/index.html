PART 1 · FEEL

蒙臉拳法

Eyes-closed feel drill. Solo only. Not sparring.

READY

05:20

清空地再開

Clear the floor. Close eyes or light cloth only. Never spar alone.

WHY

不靠眼眼先靠身

閉眼或輕布蒙一下，才聽到腳、肩、拳路。洛馬步子要穩，唔靠望住鏡。單打只做空拳，唔打人。
Close the eyes so feet, shoulder and punch path become loud. Loma feet first. Solo shadow only — nobody is the target.

HOW

  1. 清空地。腳彈開，手護下巴。
    Open floor. Bounce. Hands high.
  2. 閉眼架勢 40s。感覺兩腳都卸得走。
    Eyes closed. Feel both feet can unload.
  3. 慢刺 45s。肩先拳後、即收。
    Slow jab. Shoulder, fist, home.
  4. 慢 Pivot 45° 45s。轉完踏實。暈就開眼。
    Slow 45° pivot. Plant. Open eyes if dizzy.
  5. 細滑 45s。頭唔晃。
    Tiny shuffle. Head still.
  6. 開眼 20s → 閉眼組合 60s → 收工。
    Eyes open 20s, then closed combo 60s, then stop.

SAFETY

只可單打、空地、閉眼或輕布。唔好膠眼、唔好對打、唔好近牆、器材、樓梯。暈就停，企穩再開眼。
Solo, open floor, eyes closed or light cloth only. No tape, no sparring, no walls or machines nearby. Stop if dizzy. Sit before you walk.

呢個不代替今日洛馬 15 + A–E。練完再做，或恢復日做。
This does not replace today's Loma 15 + A–E. Do it after the session, or on recovery day.
