One hand glued to the face. The other punches and comes home.

超哥 / CHAO

一手護臉，一手出拳

蒙臉唔係閉眼。係一隻手始終蓋住臉、下巴、面顏；另一隻手先出拳，打完即收。唔好兩隻手一齊離開臉。眼張開，由護手入面望出去。
Not eyes-closed. One hand stays on the face, cheek and chin. The other punches and returns. Never both hands leave. Eyes open, looking through the guard.

HOW

  1. 架勢。後手貼下巴同面顏，射近頭。前手放眼前。
    Stance. Rear hand glued to chin and cheek. Lead hand at the eyes.
  2. 前手刺。後手唔都譲開臉。刺完前手即收。
    Jab with the lead. Rear hand never leaves the face. Snap the jab home.
  3. 後手直拳。前手即時蓋住臉。唔好一齊掉。
    Cross with the rear. Lead hand covers the face at the same time. Do not drop both.
  4. 1–2。出邊隻，另一隻就蒙臉。
    One-two. The hand that is not punching covers the face.
  5. 換手再做一輪。相同規則。
    Swap the covering hand. Same rule.

RULE

記住：一隻手攻擊，另一隻手蒙臉。唔好兩隻手離開臉。唔好掉下巴。單打空拳，唔打人。
One punches, one covers. Never both hands off the face. Never drop the chin. Solo shadow only.
