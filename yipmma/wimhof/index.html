DAILY · 5:00

Wim Hof 呼吸

5-minute daily protocol · short and steady, not a compressed 3–4 round session.

READY

05:00

坐穩或躺好再開

Sit or lie down first. Never stand, shower, or drive.

TODAY’S RULE

短而穩定 · Short and steady

不把完整 3–4 輪硬壓進 5 分鐘。今日 2 輪就算完成。冷水不算入這 5 分鐘。
Do not squeeze a full session into five minutes. Two rounds count. Cold water is separate.

PREP · 15s

寬鬆衣服，腹部可以起伏。閉眼，肩膊放下。只講一句：「今天只練 5 分鐘，不硬撑。」
Loose clothes, belly free. Eyes closed, shoulders down. One line: “Five minutes only. No forcing.”

ROUND · 約 2:15

  1. 30 次深呼吸 · 60–70s
    Inhale nose or mouth, belly out, fill. Exhale softly — do not empty hard. Steady to 30.
  2. 空肺屏氣 · 30–45s
    After breath 30, stop empty. When the urge is strong, tap 「想吸了」. Do not race the clock.
  3. 恢復呼吸 · 15s
    Big inhale, hold ~15s, soft exhale.

第 2 輪重複。這輪通常更平靜，屏氣可以稍長，仍以身體訊號為準。
Repeat. Round 2 is usually quieter. A longer hold is fine if the body agrees.

CLOSE · 15s

自然呼吸。感受胸口、手腳溫度或刺痛是否消退。起身前坐穩 5–10 秒。
Normal breath. Let chest and limb tingling fade. Sit 5–10s before standing.

COLD · NOT IN THIS TIMER

浴浴最後 30 秒冷水

不佔這 5 分鐘。水下去用平常放慢呼吸，不要做連續深呼吸。放鬆下巴與肩。一週後可加到 45–60 秒。
After the timer. Last 30s of the normal shower on cold. Slow ordinary breath — never the 30-breath pattern under water. After a week, 45–60s if it feels easy.

SAFETY

只能坐或躺。絕不在浴浴、開車、站著做呼吸。頭暈、手麻屬常見；不適就改 20 次，或當天只做 1 輪。
Sit or lie only. Never in the shower, a car, or standing. Dizzy or tingling hands: drop to 20 breaths, or stop after one round.

最佳剛起床空腹。晚上做就降強度，避免難睡。連續 7 天後才考慮某一天升級 3 輪。
Best on waking, empty stomach. Evening: easier still, so sleep is not wired. After 7 straight days, maybe add a 3-round morning.

CUE

「吸——放——」數到 30 →「停」→ 想吸了就「大口吸，停 15，放」。
“In — out —” to 30 → “hold” → urge to breathe → “big in, hold 15, release.”
